# Community code of conduct

Treat participants with respect regardless of experience, identity or background.
Discuss ideas and code constructively, accept good-faith feedback, and give credit
for other people's work. Respect maintainers' and contributors' time and boundaries.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory abuse, sexualized conduct, doxxing, and
publication of another person's private information are not acceptable. This
policy applies to repository issues, pull requests and other project-run spaces.

Report concerns privately to **conduct@tendstack.com**. Include links and relevant
context, avoiding unnecessary personal information. Reports will be handled as
privately as practicable; information may be shared where needed to investigate
or protect participants. No response-time guarantee is promised.

Maintainers may request changes, remove content, close a discussion, restrict
participation or ban an account depending on the severity and pattern of conduct.
A person involved in a report should not decide its outcome. The reporter or
subject may request reconsideration through the same private contact address.
